The Grid Standard

Engineered for High-Density Living.

How we solve the four biggest headaches of buying solid wood furniture in urban India.

01

Compact Savvy

Engineered for high-rise apartment footprints (750 to 2200 sq.ft) with zero visual bulk, tapered undercuts, and hydraulic storage to maximize floor area.

02

Heirloom Joinery

Traditional blind mortise-and-tenon joints combined with German Blum concealed runners and heavy-duty structural steel fasteners that never loosen.

03

Monsoon Resilient

Vacuum-pressure treated and seasoned strictly down to 8–10% moisture content in Jaipur kilns to prevent warping across extreme Delhi winters and humid monsoons.

04

Custom Core

We believe every floor plan has quirks. Millimeter-level sizing adjustments for odd structural columns, beams, and niches are our standard practice.
